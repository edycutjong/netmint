The 30-second path · no login, no wallet

One click. One verdict. The two rows that prove it.

NetMint turns CoinMarketCap’s daily supply history for tokenised stocks into net creation per wrapper, at market price, and puts each wrapper’s window and its largest step days on trial against the chain before it stamps a verdict.

Run it liveOpen the NVDAB 30-day lookup opens in a new tab↗

Opens /u/NVDA?from=2026-10-22&to=2026-11-21 in the app. The page calls /api/lookup on load (cold: 5 CMC calls, 7 credits; median about 2.0 s over all 20 bench runs (21 Nov, one machine); a repeat within 60 s is labelled cached). The holder panel makes its own /api/holders request.

The 7 CMC endpoints

Five on every lookup; the holder count serves the holder panel on /u/<ticker>, the key info serves /api/health.

  1. GET /v5/real-world-assets/assets/listthe universe: underlyings → rwa_id
  2. GET /v5/real-world-assets/quotes/latestevery wrapper of one stock: crypto id, issuer, price
  3. GET /v2/cryptocurrency/quotes/historicalthe daily supply, price and volume series: the engine
  4. GET /v2/cryptocurrency/infocontract addresses per chain, for the mint check
  5. GET /v2/cryptocurrency/quotes/latesttoday’s point (shown apart, never in the window) + volume
  6. GET /v1/dex/holders/countholder count per chain (holder panel)
  7. GET /v1/key/infoplan and credits for /api/health

Honest limits

  • After 9 Dec 23:59 UTC the event key is expected to revert to CMC’s free Basic tier. A click still calls CMC live; if CMC refuses a call on plan, a stored dated body answers it: the result line says live-stored, and the receipt shows the stored body with its capture date. Tested with a simulated plan refusal only; it has not yet run against a real Basic key.
  • Unread chains. NetMint reads BNB Chain and Ethereum. Supply on other chains (Solana, Arbitrum, Base, Robinhood Chain, X Layer and more) stays NOT VERIFIABLE.
  • Dollars are net creation at market price, not money that changed hands.
