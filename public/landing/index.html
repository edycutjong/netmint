Questions a judge would ask

Is the click really live?

Yes. Each ticker button calls /api/lookup on this domain; the server calls CoinMarketCap with its own key (never sent to your browser) and returns every receipt. The panel prints mode, credits and time; a repeat within 60 s says cached. After 9 Dec 23:59 UTC the event key is expected to revert to CMC’s free plan: if CMC then refuses a call with a plan or credit error, a stored dated CMC body answers it and the receipt says so (mode live-stored); a window outside the stored bodies shows CMC’s error, not a guess.

What is replayed, not live?

The top leaf and the trial book replay committed captures (2026-11-21 01:40 UTC and 02:53 UTC). Chain values come from a committed daily totalSupply baseline read from archive RPC; a day it lacks is read live. A live re-run at 04:46 UTC printed the same +$202.83M.

Is net creation money that buyers put in?

No. It is units created minus redeemed, times that day’s market price, counted like an ETF flow table. A mint is a transfer from the zero address; every sampled NVDAB mint went to one address, consistent with issuer-level creation, not buyer data.

What does “within remedy” mean?

In the Trial of the Pyx, documented since 1248, a jury checks the Royal Mint’s coins within a legal tolerance called the remedy. Here the chain’s change must match CMC’s within ±10% (or 0.5% of supply for a small move) before a move is struck REAL INFLOW or OUTFLOW.

Why are 107 wrappers not verifiable?

Their supply sits on chains NetMint does not read yet (for example Solana, Arbitrum, Base, Robinhood Chain, X Layer), or the move may sit there. NetMint prints NOT VERIFIABLE instead of guessing.

What happens when the event key reverts?

It reverts to CMC’s free Basic tier when submissions close (9 Dec 2026, 23:59 UTC). If CMC then refuses on plan, the click answers live-stored: stored dated CMC bodies plus chain reads, labelled. Tested offline; not yet run against a Basic key.
