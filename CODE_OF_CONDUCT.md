# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Backing a claim with a receipt (a CoinMarketCap response, a chain read, a test) instead of an assertion

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, publishing others'
private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Report unacceptable behaviour privately through GitHub:
**Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository (it reaches the maintainer only), or open a minimal issue asking for
private contact. Reports are reviewed and answered promptly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.
